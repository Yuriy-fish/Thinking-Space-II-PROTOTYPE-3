## 1. EXECUTIVE STATEMENT ON AI INVOLVEMENT

## 2. SCOPE OF AI CONTRIBUTIONS

Development was accelerated and augmented through the use of **Antigravity**, an agentic AI assistant developed by Google DeepMind. Specific tasks conducted with AI assistance include:

1. **System Architecture & Code Scaffolding:**
   - Generation of modular ES Module structure, component hierarchy, client-side routing, and responsive CSS token design systems.
   - Translation of system requirements into clean, zero-dependency browser and Node.js code.

2. **Mathematical & Algorithmic Formulation:**
   - Implementation of Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) comparison functions (continuous tolerance envelopes, categorical Jaccard tokenization, cyclic angular divergence, and dynamic weight renormalization).
   - Implementation of explainable machine learning routines: 9D normalized feature extraction, explainable K-Means clustering with deterministic k-means++ seeding, statistical Euclidean anomaly detection, and non-dominated Pareto frontier sorting.
   - Graph algorithmic routines: Breadth-First Search (BFS) multi-hop indirect pathway traversal and Cytoscape.js export formatting.

3. **Automated Testing & Quality Assurance:**
   - Authoring of 6 comprehensive automated test suites covering 120 unit tests with zero external runtime dependencies.
   - Cross-browser DOM verification via headless browser automation.

4. **Documentation & Specification Authoring:**
   - Technical drafting of architectural specifications, data dictionaries, validation reports, and user guides.

## 3. STRICT AI LIMITATIONS & SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY CONTROLS

To preserve data provenance and scientific authenticity, strict safeguards were enforced throughout development:

1. **No Synthetic or Hallucinated Data:**
   - The AI was **strictly prohibited from inventing, fabricating, or simulating scientific observations, mineral compositions, or physical telemetry**.
   - All planetary coordinates (Moon and Mars) and Earth analog locations are sourced from documented NASA PDS, USGS Astrogeology, and peer-reviewed literature.
   - When parameter measurements were missing or uncertain, values were recorded as explicit `null` rather than estimated through generative extrapolation.

2. **No Black-Box Machine Learning:**
   - Deep neural networks were intentionally excluded in favor of **explainable, deterministic algorithms** (MCDA, explainable K-Means, distance metrics).
   - All ML clusters generate human-readable dominant feature summaries and carry mandatory scientific caveats stating that clusters represent algorithmic groupings, not confirmed scientific analogs.

3. **No Automated Origin Assumption (Equifinality Safeguard):**
   - The system incorporates active checks for **morphological equifinality** (e.g., distinguishing kinetic impact craters from circular alkaline magmatic domes or volcanic calderas), preventing false-positive astrobiological candidate selections.
